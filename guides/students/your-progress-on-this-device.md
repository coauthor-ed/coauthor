CoAuthor has no accounts and no passwords. You do not sign up anywhere. Your name, your progress and your test results are saved **on this device only**, inside the browser.

## What this means for you

- Nobody outside your home can see your progress. It is not sent to any website.
- The app works without the internet once it has loaded, so you can study offline.
- Your progress does **not** move to another phone or computer by itself.

## Keep it safe

Your progress can be lost if:

- someone clears the browser's data or history for this site;
- you use a private or incognito window (it forgets everything when you close it);
- the device is reset or replaced.

To be safe, ask a grown-up to save a backup now and then. It takes a few seconds. They can see how in [Backing up and moving progress](backing-up-progress).

![The Backup and restore window in the parent area, with an Export backup button and an Import backup button.](media/progress-backup.png "A grown-up makes a backup here, in the parent area.")

> [!TIP]
> Always open CoAuthor in the same browser on the same device. A different browser on the same computer has its own, separate copy of the app.

## Sharing a device

If brothers and sisters share one device, each person should have their own profile, so progress does not get mixed up. Use the name button in the top bar to switch profile.
